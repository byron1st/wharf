What a sync does

  • Imports Jira issues assigned to you that aren’t done, and updates the ones already here.
  • Refreshes Service Desk requests. Done and Canceled requests drop out of Today.
  • Only reads from Atlassian. Todos, notes and links you add in Wharf are never written back.
  • Issues resolved or unassigned in Jira aren’t fetched again, so they stay here as last synced until you remove them.
