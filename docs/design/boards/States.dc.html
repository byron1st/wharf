Behavior
  • One popover per field. Start, End and Due each open their own calendar under the field.
  • Picking a day saves and closes. No separate Save button.
  • Quick picks sit above the calendar: Today, Tomorrow, this Fri, Next Mon.
  • The paired date is outlined (Start when picking End, Due when picking Start) and the range between them is tinted.
  • Days that would break the order are disabled — End can’t come before Start, Start can’t come after Due.
  • Today keeps the orange ring used everywhere else. Weeks start on Monday.
  • Clear removes the date. It’s disabled when the field is already empty.
  • Keyboard: arrows move by day, PgUp/PgDn by month, Enter picks, Esc closes.

Behavior
  • “Add reminder” opens presets first. One click on a preset adds it and closes.
  • Presets are relative to now and to the todo’s End date. “Morning of end date” only shows when End is set.
  • Below the presets, a calendar and a 24-hour time field make a custom reminder. Past days are disabled.
  • Clicking an existing reminder opens the same form to edit it, with Delete on the left.
  • Time is typed as HH:MM. A time already passed today turns the field red and keeps the button disabled.
  • A reminder that already went off turns gray with a check and “Sent”. It can’t be edited, only removed. In the Today list the Reminder column shows the next upcoming one, or “Sent HH:MM” when none are left.
  • Reminders keep their violet color everywhere: selected day, list rows, chips and the bell icon.
